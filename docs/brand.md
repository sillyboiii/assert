# Assert — Product Brief

**One-liner:** Stake real money on your own goals. Hit them and win — bail and your friend earns it.

**What it is:** A commitment app on the Base blockchain. Users bet their own money on personal goals (fitness, shipping a project, quitting a habit) with a trusted friend as *referee*. The smart contract holds the money and enforces the rules — no human accounts, no fees, no trust needed. Discipline with teeth.

**Core flow (3 steps):**
1. **Create** — set a goal, a stake ($5–$500), a deadline, and pick a referee friend.
2. **Referee accepts** — friend gets an invite, joins, and signs the role onchain.
3. **Settlement** — goal met → referee approves → money unlocks back to you. Deadline passes unverified → money auto-pays out to the referee. It's binary, transparent, and enforced by code.

**Key screens:** Goal creation form · Referee invite · Live commitment card (countdown, stake, status) · Settlement results (win / bust) · Dashboard of past commitments.

**Design direction:** Trust + grit. Editorial and high-contrast, like a premium betting card meets a mountain tracker. Bold numerals, big deadlines, status colors: **approve = green**, **deadline bust = red**, **pending = warm amber**. Neutral base (ink black / bone white) so the stakes feel real. Logo mark: a hard checkmark or a fist pressed into a seal — *the gesture of swearing on it.* The wordmark is **Assert** — tagline **"Assert it. Or fold."**
